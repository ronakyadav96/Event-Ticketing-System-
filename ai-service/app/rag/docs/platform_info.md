# Platform Information, Customer Support & Buyer Guarantee

## 1. About the Platform
Our Event Ticketing System is an end-to-end intelligent entertainment platform connecting millions of fans to live music concerts, stand-up comedy specials, blockbuster cinema screenings, and cultural exhibitions across the nation.

## 2. Customer Support Channels & Operating Hours
We are committed to providing immediate, round-the-clock assistance:
- **AI Chatbot Assistant:** Available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year** directly inside the web and mobile applications for instant booking status checks, seat queries, venue guides, and automated cancellations.
- **Human Support Representatives:** Dedicated customer care specialists are available daily from **8:00 AM to 11:00 PM IST** (including weekends and public holidays).
- **Contact Email:** For complex billing issues, corporate bulk bookings, or partnership queries, reach us at:
  - Customer Inquiries: `support@eventticketing.internal`
  - Organizer Partnerships: `partners@eventticketing.internal`
- **Toll-Free Phone Helpline:** `+91-1800-200-8455` or shortcode `+91-800-TICKETS` (operational 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM IST).
- **Average Response Time:** Chatbot responses are instantaneous (< 1 second); email tickets are answered within 2 hours during operating hours.

## 3. The 100% Buyer Guarantee
Every ticket purchased through our platform is backed by our comprehensive Buyer Protection Guarantee:
- **Authenticity Assured:** Every ticket is generated directly through verified venue and artist box-office integrations. We guarantee 100% authentic QR codes and valid gate entry.
- **On-Time Delivery:** Your digital tickets will always be accessible and delivered prior to the scheduled showtime.
- **Full Refund on Cancellation:** In the event an organizer cancels or permanently calls off a show, your purchase is automatically protected with a 100% full refund to your original payment method.

## 4. Verified Organizer Standards
All event creators, festival promoters, and venues on our platform must complete comprehensive KYC verification, legal compliance vetting, and venue safety certifications before publishing events to our public catalog.

## 5. Security & Privacy
We adhere strictly to ISO 27001 and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance standards. All customer financial data and payment credentials are encrypted using AES-256 bit encryption and processed through certified payment gateway infrastructure.
