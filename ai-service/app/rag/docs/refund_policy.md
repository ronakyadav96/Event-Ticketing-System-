# Official Refund Policy & Processing Guidelines

## 1. Overview of Refund Policy
Our refund policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and prompt. Refund eligibility depends on whether the cancellation was initiated by the event organizer or requested by the attendee.

## 2. Event Cancellations & Auto-Refunds
- **Organizer-Cancelled Events:** If an event, concert, or movie screening is cancelled by the event organizer, artist, or venue authorities, all ticket holders are entitled to a **100% full refund**.
- **Automatic Processing:** You do not need to submit a manual support ticket or refund request for cancelled events. Our platform triggers an automated batch refund within **24 to 48 hours** of the official cancellation announcement.
- **Components Covered:** The automatic cancellation refund covers the entire ticket base amount as well as applicable venue facility fees and taxes.

## 3. Rescheduled & Postponed Events
- **Date or Venue Changes:** When an event is rescheduled to a new date, time, or venue, existing tickets automatically remain valid for the new date.
- **Refund Window for Rescheduled Shows:** Attendees who cannot attend the rescheduled date have a **72-hour grace period** from the announcement date to claim a full refund via the AI Assistant or customer support. After this window closes, standard cancellation terms apply.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines & Methods
- **Initiation Window:** Approved refund requests are processed and sent to banking networks within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **Bank Settlement Times:** Depending on your financial institution:
  - **UPI Payments:** Typically credited within **24 hours**.
  - **Debit / Credit Cards:** Credited within **5 to 7 business days** (excluding weekends and bank holidays).
  - **Net Banking:** Credited within **3 to 6 business days**.
- **Destination Account:** All refunds are strictly credited back to the original payment source (card, UPI ID, or bank account) used at checkout. Cash refunds are never issued.

## 5. Non-Refundable Charges & Partial Refunds
- **Convenience / Platform Fees:** For voluntary user-initiated cancellations, third-party payment gateway convenience fees and platform processing fees are non-refundable.
- **Physical Merchandise & Add-ons:** Commemorative merchandise or physical memorabilia badges that have already been shipped or fulfilled prior to the cancellation request cannot be refunded.
- **No-Show Policy:** Attendees who do not arrive at the venue or fail to cancel before the specified cancellation window are not eligible for a refund.
