# Event Ticketing Platform - Booking FAQ & Guidelines

## 1. Seat Selection Rules
- **Interactive Seat Map:** For reserved-seating events (such as stadium concerts, auditorium comedy specials, and cinema screenings), customers can select specific seats directly on the interactive floor layout.
- **Seat Lock Mechanism:** When you select your seats and proceed to checkout, those seats are temporarily held under a **10-minute hold lock**. If payment is not completed within 10 minutes, the lock is automatically released back to the general inventory.
- **Contiguous Seating Rule:** To avoid single orphaned seats in auditoriums, bookings leaving an isolated single empty seat between selected seats and booked seats may be restricted during peak periods.

## 2. Booking Limits & Restrictions
- **Maximum Tickets Per Transaction:** To prevent scalping and ensure fair access, customers can purchase a maximum of **10 tickets per order** for standard events, and **4 to 6 tickets per order** for high-demand concerts and exclusive premieres.
- **Multiple Transactions:** Users cannot place concurrent booking requests for the same event from the same account while a cart session is active.

## 3. Supported Payment Methods
- **Accepted Modes:** We support all major Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Debit Cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 50+ major banks, and Stripe Checkout.
- **Currencies Supported:** Primary transactions are processed in Indian Rupees (INR). International credit cards are accepted via the Stripe gateway with currency auto-conversion.
- **Payment Failures & Auto-Reversal:** If funds are debited from your bank account but the booking fails to generate a ticket due to gateway timeout, the transaction is marked as failed and funds are automatically refunded to your original source account within 3 to 5 business days.

## 4. Digital Ticket Delivery
- **Instant Delivery:** Once payment is confirmed, an official E-Ticket is immediately dispatched to your registered email address and mobile phone via SMS/WhatsApp.
- **In-App & Chatbot Access:** You can also retrieve your active ticket anytime by asking the AI Assistant ("Show my booking status") or viewing the "My Bookings" tab on the user dashboard.
- **Ticket Format:** Digital tickets include a cryptographically secure 2D QR Code, unique Ticket ID (e.g., `TKT-XXXXXX`), event schedule details, venue gates, and designated seat numbers.

## 5. E-Ticket Validation & Venue Access
- **QR Code Entry:** Present your digital e-ticket on your mobile device at the venue turnstiles or security scanners. Printed paper copies are also accepted but not mandatory.
- **Duplicate Scans:** Each QR code is strictly single-use. If a duplicate scan is detected at the entrance, access will only be granted to the original verified purchaser upon photo ID verification.
- **Ticket Transfers:** If transferring a ticket to a family member or friend, use the official "Transfer Ticket" button in your account dashboard at least 4 hours before gate opening.
