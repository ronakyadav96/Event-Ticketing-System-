# Venue Entry, Security & Event Attendance FAQ

## 1. Venue Entry Requirements & Gate Timings
- **Gate Opening:** Venue gates typically open **90 minutes prior** to stadium concerts and **45 minutes prior** to theatre plays, stand-up comedy shows, and cinema screenings.
- **Last Entry:** Gates close 30 minutes after the performance commences. Late arrivals may have to wait until an intermission to take their seats to avoid disrupting other patrons.
- **Entry Documents:** Attendees must present their valid E-Ticket (printed or mobile QR code) along with matching identification.

## 2. Mandatory Identification (ID Proof) Policies
- **Accepted Photo IDs:** Attendees must carry an official government-issued photo ID. Accepted forms include:
  - Aadhaar Card
  - Passport
  - Driver's License
  - Voter ID Card
  - Student / College ID (applicable only for discounted student passes)
- **Primary Booker Verification:** For group bookings, the primary purchaser whose name is printed on the master booking must be present at the gate or must have officially transferred tickets through the platform.

## 3. Age Restrictions & Classification
- **Concerts & Music Festivals:** Children under 12 years of age are generally not permitted unless specified as a family show. Minors between 12 and 16 years must be accompanied by a ticket-holding adult (18+).
- **Stand-Up Comedy Shows:** Most comedy performances enforce a strict **16+ or 18+ age restriction** due to mature content and adult language.
- **Movie Screenings:** Adherence to CBFC ratings is mandatory:
  - **A (Adults Only):** Strictly 18+ only. Valid ID proof of age is verified at cinema podiums.
  - **U/A (Parental Guidance):** Children under 12 must be accompanied by parents.
  - **U (Universal):** Unrestricted public exhibition suitable for all ages.

## 4. Prohibited Items Inside Venues
To ensure attendee safety and security, security personnel will conduct mandatory bag checks and metal detector screenings at all gates. The following items are strictly prohibited:
- **Outside Food & Beverages:** Outside snacks, beverages, fast food, and alcohol are strictly forbidden. Free drinking water stations and commercial concessions are available inside all venues. Exceptions are made solely for infant formula and prescribed medical dietary items.
- **Professional Recording Equipment:** DSLR cameras, detachable telephoto lenses, GoPros, tripods, selfie sticks, and professional audio/video recording rigs are banned. Smartphone cameras for personal use are permitted.
- **Dangerous Objects:** Sharp items, knives, scissors, firearms, fireworks, lasers, pepper spray, and flammable materials.
- **Smoking & Vaping:** Cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vape pens, and lighters are prohibited inside auditorium seating and enclosed halls. Designated smoking zones are provided outside stadium perimeters where permissible.
- **Large Backpacks & Luggage:** Bags larger than 30cm x 30cm x 15cm must be deposited at venue cloakrooms.

## 5. Parking Facilities & Transportation
- **On-Site Parking:** Most indoor arenas, multiplexes, and mall venues (e.g., PVR Phoenix Palladium, Inorbit Malad, Cinepolis Viviana) offer multi-level paid vehicle parking with EV charging stations.
- **Stadium Event Advisory:** For major stadium concerts at DY Patil Stadium or JLN Stadium, parking is extremely limited and subject to traffic diversions. Attendees are strongly encouraged to use metro transit, suburban rail, or rideshare services (Uber/Ola).
