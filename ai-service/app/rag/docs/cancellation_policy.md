# Ticket Cancellation Policy & Cutoff Windows

## 1. User-Initiated Cancellations
Customers can request ticket cancellations directly through the AI Chatbot Assistant by stating "I want to cancel my booking" or by navigating to the "My Bookings" section on their profile.

## 2. Cancellation Cutoff Window & Deadlines
- **Eligible Cancellation Period:** Ticket cancellations are permitted only if requested **at least 24 hours prior** to the published show start time.
- **Strict 24-Hour Cutoff Rule:** **No cancellations or refunds are permitted within 24 hours of the event.** For example, if your show is scheduled for 7:00 PM today, attempting to cancel 2 hours, 6 hours, or 12 hours before the show will be rejected by the system.
- **Why the Cutoff Exists:** Within 24 hours of a performance, artist fees, security staffing, and venue catering commitments are finalized, and released seats cannot reasonably be re-marketed to other fans.

## 3. Cancellation Deductions & Tiers
For cancellations made outside the 24-hour cutoff window:
- **More than 7 days prior to event:** 90% refund of the ticket base price (10% standard processing fee).
- **Between 48 hours and 7 days prior to event:** 75% refund of the ticket base price (25% cancellation charge).
- **Between 24 hours and 48 hours prior to event:** 50% refund of the ticket base price (50% cancellation charge).
- **Less than 24 hours prior to event:** 0% refund (strictly non-refundable).

## 4. Organizer-Initiated Cancellations & Force Majeure
- **Artist Sickness / Technical Failures:** In the rare event that an organizer or venue operator cancels an event due to weather, technical failure, or artist unavailability, the 24-hour cutoff rule is waived.
- **Immediate Status Update:** All ticket holders receive instant SMS and email notifications, and automated 100% refunds are triggered without requiring customer action.

## 5. Non-Cancellable Ticket Types
- **Flash Sale & Early Bird Passes:** Tickets explicitly tagged as "Non-Refundable / Final Sale" during promotional flash sales cannot be cancelled voluntarily.
- **VIP Meet & Greet Experiences:** Due to limited artist access quotas, VIP packages cannot be cancelled within 14 days of the event.
